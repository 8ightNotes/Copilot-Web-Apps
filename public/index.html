            ? How to Play

            🕵️ The Setup

            You are secretly assigned a role in a small neighborhood. Each role has a hidden objective you must complete before the day ends.

            🎯 Objectives

            Use your actions each turn to talk with people, move around, and use your special ability. Build trust — or break it.

            ⚡ Abilities

            Your role gives you a unique ability. Use it wisely — some require a target nearby, and all take time.

            👁 Suspicion

            NPCs remember what you do. Unusual behavior raises suspicion. If trust falls too low, your cover may be blown.

            🏆 Winning

            Complete your objective before the day ends. The challenge is to do it without arousing too much suspicion.
