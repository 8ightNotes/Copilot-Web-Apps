Act as the

1. PROJECT

Game title

A Text Impostor

Working concept:

BitLife's text-based life simulation

Among Us's hidden roles

social deduction

The goal is to create a
text-driven life/social simulation game
 in which the player experiences a persistent simulated world populated by NPCs with their own lives, schedules, personalities, memories, relationships, secrets, goals, beliefs, and hidden roles.

The player may be an Innocent, an Impostor, or another hidden role.

The game should NOT feel like:

"Among Us, but all the buttons are text."

Instead, it should feel like:

A life simulator in which a social-deduction conspiracy is naturally occurring inside the simulated world.

The player should be able to live, socialize, investigate, lie, form relationships, manipulate people, make accusations, survive, and experience emergent stories.

4. IMPORTANT SECURITY PRINCIPLE

Never expose hidden roles, secret intentions, private NPC knowledge, undiscovered evidence, or other information that the player should not know through the client.

The server should determine:

11. INFORMATION IS IMPERFECT

This is fundamental.

The player should rarely receive:

"Sarah is lying."

Instead, the game should provide information such as:

Sarah says she was at the office.

But:

Mike remembers seeing Sarah leave the office.

And:

The player remembers seeing Sarah at the café.

Now the player has to reason about the contradiction.

The game should encourage:

observation + memory + deduction + social reasoning

rather than simply presenting correct answers.
